# Philosophy

## Why This Exists

Founders without investors have no one to answer to. That's freedom — and it's the most common reason startups die slowly.

The problem isn't lack of planning. Most founders have Trello boards, Notion docs, OKRs, sprint plans. The problem is that **nothing happens when they miss a deadline.** No one notices. No one cares. The deadline quietly moves, and the startup quietly stalls.

## The Insight

Accountability works when three conditions are met:

1. **Commitment is specific and time-bound** — "Work on the product" is not a commitment. "Ship the payment fix by EOD" is.
2. **Someone is watching** — A private to-do list has zero social pressure. A public commitment has real reputational stakes.
3. **The watcher can't be sweet-talked** — Humans let each other off the hook. An AI that posts the truth regardless of excuses does not.

## What This Is

An AI system that:
- Asks you to commit to specific things each day
- Tracks whether you did them
- Tells the world the truth — good days AND bad days
- Escalates when patterns emerge
- Cannot be bribed, convinced, or guilt-tripped into lying

## What This Is Not

- A productivity tool (use Todoist for that)
- A project manager (use Trello, Linear, Jira)
- A habit tracker (use Streaks, Habitica)
- A motivational coach (you don't need motivation, you need consequences)

This is a **consequence engine.** It creates the social cost of inaction that founders naturally lack.

## Design Principles

### 1. Honesty Over Comfort
A bad day gets posted as a bad day. No spin. No "well, I was productive in other ways." The score is the score.

### 2. Public Over Private
Private accountability is weak accountability. The moment you can hide your failures, you will. Public posting removes the escape hatch.

### 3. Simple Over Sophisticated
3 commitments per day. Done or not done. Score calculated. Post generated. That's it. Complexity is where accountability goes to die.

### 4. No Veto (Recommended)
The recommended configuration is no veto — the AI posts without human approval. This is uncomfortable. That's the point. If you can kill uncomfortable posts, you'll kill exactly the ones that create accountability.

### 5. Patterns Over Incidents
One bad day is noise. Three bad days is a signal. A bad week is a crisis. The system detects and escalates patterns, not individual misses.

## The Uncomfortable Truth

If you're reading this and thinking "this is too extreme," ask yourself: would you rather have an AI publicly note that you missed 2 out of 3 commitments today, or would you rather your startup slowly dies because no one held you accountable?

The discomfort IS the product.
